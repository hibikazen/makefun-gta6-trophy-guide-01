    How to use the tracker

    • Tick a trophy’s box when you earn it. Progress saves in this browser automatically.
    • Counted trophies (stunts, vehicles, meditations…) have a counter in their guide — set it as you go.
    • Export downloads your progress as a file; Import reads one back, on this or another computer.
    • Hide story spoilers keeps the 12 story trophies’ names and descriptions hidden until you earn them.
    • Everything runs from these files alone. The app fetches nothing — the only network use is a source link you choose to follow.
